    Process

    HOW
    IT
    WORKS

    From registration to reward in three clear steps. No ambiguity, no delays.

    01
    Choose a target & start hunting
    Browse active programs from verified Egyptian and Arab companies. Each program defines its scope clearly — Web, API, or Mobile. Pick your specialty and begin testing within defined boundaries.
    02
    Submit your finding
    Document the vulnerability with reproduction steps and submit through our structured form. Our AI engine immediately classifies severity, checks for duplicates, and validates scope — cutting review time from weeks to minutes.

    Once verified by the company, your reward is released instantly. Your profile accumulates verified findings, building a credible track record that opens doors to private programs and higher-value targets.
